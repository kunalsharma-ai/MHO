1. Welcome to Our Healthcare Professionals Panel

Welcome to the Frequent Research Healthcare Professionals Panel, a research community for qualified and eligible healthcare professionals who contribute their knowledge, professional experience and opinions to healthcare-related research.

The panel may include, but is not limited to:

Medical doctors, physicians and medical specialists.

Surgeons and other specialist practitioners.

Dentists and dental professionals.

Nurses, midwives and nursing professionals.

Pharmacists and pharmacy professionals.

Laboratory, pathology and diagnostic professionals.

Allied health professionals and therapists.

Radiology, imaging and medical technology professionals.

Public health professionals and epidemiologists.

Healthcare researchers, academics and clinical research professionals.

Hospital administrators, healthcare managers and healthcare IT professionals.

Healthcare support professionals and other eligible medical-sector personnel.

Our research may cover healthcare practices, medicines, medical devices, diagnostics, patient care, healthcare technology, medical education, hospital operations, public health, professional experiences and industry developments.

Participation may involve online surveys, telephone interviews, expert discussions, product evaluations, concept testing or other research activities.

Membership is voluntary and does not establish a clinical, employment, consulting or advisory relationship with Frequent Research or its clients.

2. Eligibility and Professional Qualification

To participate, you must meet the applicable eligibility requirements and provide accurate information about your professional background.

Depending on the study, eligibility may require:

A relevant healthcare qualification, professional registration or licence.

Current or recent employment, practice or experience in a specified healthcare role.

A relevant speciality, department, clinical setting or area of professional expertise.

Experience with particular medicines, medical devices, diagnostics, treatments or healthcare systems.

A specified level of professional seniority or decision-making responsibility.

Compliance with applicable professional, ethical, employer and legal requirements.

Not every study requires a medical licence. Eligibility will depend on the participant category and the research objectives.

You must not claim qualifications, registrations, clinical experience, prescribing authority or professional responsibilities that you do not possess.

We may conduct proportionate verification using professional registration details, work email addresses, publicly available professional directories or other appropriate methods. Where necessary, we may request supporting information, explaining why it is needed and how it will be handled.

We will not require unnecessary copies of licences, identity documents or employment records merely because you join the panel.

Membership does not guarantee eligibility for every study, a minimum number of invitations or payment for participation.

3. Information We May Collect

We may collect the following information depending on your registration, professional category and study requirements.

A. Registration and contact information

Full name and preferred display name.

Email address and telephone number.

Country, state, city or professional location.

Preferred language and communication preferences.

Date of birth or age confirmation where necessary.

Account credentials and account security information.

B. Healthcare professional profile

Professional designation, qualification and speciality.

Professional registration or licence information, where relevant.

Years of professional experience.

Healthcare discipline and area of practice.

Employment status and type of healthcare organisation.

Hospital, clinic, laboratory, pharmacy, university or other workplace, where relevant.

Department, professional seniority and responsibilities.

Clinical, administrative, research or operational experience.

Healthcare setting, such as public hospital, private hospital, community practice or independent practice.

Geographic area of practice and patient population characteristics, where relevant.

Involvement in prescribing, purchasing, procurement, formulary decisions, medical devices or healthcare technology, where relevant.

Professional development, research and relevant qualifications.

C. Research responses

Depending on the study, we may collect your professional opinions and responses about:

Clinical workflows and treatment practices.

Healthcare delivery and patient-care processes.

Medicine awareness, prescribing experiences and product evaluation.

Medical devices, diagnostics and healthcare technology.

Clinical research, medical education and professional development.

Healthcare budgets, procurement and purchasing decisions.

Hospital management, staffing and operational challenges.

Public health, healthcare access and system performance.

Product concepts, medical information and industry developments.

Research findings, suggestions and professional recommendations.

These responses are collected for the stated research purpose and must not include patient-identifying information or confidential information you are not authorised to disclose.

D. Technical and security information

We may collect IP address, device and browser details, login records, session information, essential cookies, survey progress and response-quality indicators.

We may use proportionate security and validation measures to prevent duplicate accounts, unauthorised access, automated submissions and suspected fraud.

Optional analytics or tracking technologies will be used according to applicable law and your cookie preferences.

E. Incentive and payment information

Where a study offers an incentive or honorarium, we may collect reward preferences, redemption history, transaction references and payment information required to administer it.

Tax or identity verification information will be requested where appropriate and legally required.

F. Sensitive personal information

Professional research may occasionally involve sensitive topics, including your own health information, professional disciplinary information or other specially protected personal data.

We will not collect such information routinely unless it is relevant to a legitimate research purpose. Where required, we will provide additional information and obtain explicit consent or establish another appropriate lawful basis before processing it.

Your consent to join the panel does not automatically authorise the collection of every category of sensitive information.

4. Purpose of Information Collection

We may use your information to:

Register and manage your panel membership.

Verify professional eligibility and relevant experience.

Match you with appropriate healthcare research studies.

Conduct healthcare market research, professional surveys and interviews.

Understand professional opinions and healthcare-sector trends.

Evaluate medicines, medical devices, diagnostics, technologies and healthcare services where the study permits.

Assess research quality and detect duplicate, inconsistent or fraudulent responses.

Administer eligible incentives and payments.

Communicate study invitations, account notices and support updates.

Improve panel operations, research methods and participant experience.

Comply with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

Protect the security and integrity of our research services.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent as provided by applicable law. Where another lawful basis applies, we will explain it in our Privacy Notice when required.

5. Voluntary Participation and Professional Independence

Your participation in the panel is voluntary.

You may accept or decline individual invitations and, where permitted by the study, skip questions or stop participating.

You agree to:

Provide truthful responses based on your professional knowledge and experience.

Participate only in studies for which you meet the stated eligibility criteria.

Disclose relevant conflicts of interest where the study requests this information.

Follow the study instructions and any applicable professional or ethical obligations.

Avoid misrepresenting your clinical experience, qualifications or workplace responsibilities.

Avoid submitting responses on behalf of another professional unless expressly authorised by the study.

You are not required to endorse any medicine, device, treatment, company or healthcare product merely because you participate in a research study.

Your answers should reflect your independent professional judgement. Participation does not imply that your employer, professional body or healthcare organisation endorses the research or any resulting findings.

6. Patient Privacy and Confidentiality

Protecting patient confidentiality is a fundamental condition of participating in healthcare research through our panel.

You must not submit or disclose identifiable patient information unless a particular study has been specifically designed and authorised to collect it and all applicable legal, ethical and consent requirements have been satisfied.

Unless expressly authorised by a study’s approved procedures, do not include:

Patient names, initials or contact details.

Medical record numbers, hospital identification numbers or insurance identifiers.

Exact dates of birth or other identifying demographic combinations.

Photographs, recordings or documents that identify patients.

Clinical records, prescriptions, laboratory reports or medical images containing identifiable information.

Case descriptions that could reasonably identify a patient.

Information about a patient obtained in confidence that you are not authorised to disclose.

Where a study asks about clinical experiences, respond using appropriately anonymised, aggregated or generalised information.

Removing a patient’s name alone may not be sufficient to anonymise information. You must consider whether the individual could still be identified from the remaining details.

If you inadvertently submit identifiable patient information, notify the research team promptly. We may restrict access, remove or securely handle the information and take other appropriate steps under applicable law.

7. Confidential Healthcare Organisation Information

You must respect the confidentiality obligations applicable to your employer, hospital, clinic, laboratory, pharmacy, research institution or other healthcare organisation.

Unless you are authorised to disclose it, do not submit:

Confidential patient or employee records.

Non-public hospital financial or operational information.

Confidential procurement bids, contracts or supplier negotiations.

Restricted medicine pricing, commercial terms or unpublished purchasing arrangements.

Proprietary research data or unpublished study results.

Confidential clinical protocols or internal documents.

Trade secrets, restricted product specifications or proprietary technology.

Information subject to a non-disclosure agreement or another legal restriction.

Where a study asks about institutional practices, use information that you are authorised to share. You may decline a question where answering would breach a legal, contractual, professional or ethical obligation.

Participation does not override your duties to patients, employers, research sponsors or professional regulatory bodies.

8. Research Quality, Screening and Verification

Some studies may use screening questions to confirm professional speciality, experience, workplace setting, prescribing responsibilities or other eligibility criteria.

We may use proportionate quality checks to identify:

Duplicate accounts or repeated participation beyond permitted limits.

Inconsistent or implausible responses.

Misrepresented professional qualifications.

Automated or fraudulent submissions.

Attempts to manipulate study eligibility or incentives.

We may exclude affected submissions, request clarification or restrict an account where reasonably justified and permitted by law.

A screening-out decision does not automatically mean that you have acted improperly. Some studies have limited quotas or narrowly defined eligibility criteria.

Where practical, we will provide a way to raise questions about disputed account or incentive decisions.

9. Medicines, Medical Devices and Healthcare Product Research

Some studies may ask about medicines, medical devices, diagnostic tools, treatment pathways, medical technologies or healthcare products.

You agree to:

Respond according to your genuine professional experience and the study’s instructions.

Distinguish personal professional opinion from established clinical evidence where relevant.

Avoid disclosing patient-identifying information or restricted clinical data.

Avoid making unauthorised promotional claims on behalf of a manufacturer or sponsor.

Disclose relevant conflicts of interest where requested and applicable.

Follow any study-specific confidentiality, safety or regulatory instructions.

Research participation does not constitute an endorsement of a product and does not replace independent clinical judgement.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, the panel is not a clinical trial recruitment service, treatment service, medical advice service or substitute for approved clinical research procedures.

If a study involves clinical trials, regulated medical research, medical device investigations or other activities requiring specific approvals, the relevant study must operate under its applicable authorisations and procedures. These general panel terms do not replace those requirements.

10. Product Safety and Adverse Event Reporting

Certain medicine, vaccine, medical device or other healthcare product studies may ask about safety observations, adverse events, product complaints or device malfunctions.

Where relevant, the study instructions will explain how such information must be reported and whether additional contact or follow-up may be required.

If you become aware of a potential adverse event, product complaint or device malfunction during a study:

Follow the study-specific reporting instructions.

Do not delay any professional or legal reporting obligation that applies to you.

Do not include unnecessary patient-identifying information in a general survey response.

Provide only information you are authorised to disclose through the designated reporting process.

Where applicable, safety reports may need to be passed to a study sponsor, manufacturer or authorised safety team to meet legal or regulatory obligations.

The panel’s general support team is not a substitute for emergency medical services, clinical advice or an authorised pharmacovigilance reporting channel.

11. Research Clients, Sponsors and Information Sharing

We may work with pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, healthcare providers, research agencies, diagnostic companies, technology providers, public health organisations and other legitimate research clients.

Where necessary and lawful, information may be shared with:

Research clients and study sponsors.

Healthcare research agencies and authorised project partners.

Survey hosting and panel management providers.

Professional verification and fraud-prevention services.

Cloud hosting, security and technical support providers.

Incentive and payment providers.

Auditors, professional advisers and public authorities where legally required.

We will apply appropriate safeguards to information handled by service providers acting on our behalf.

Where possible and appropriate, research outputs will use aggregated, statistical, pseudonymised or anonymised information.

Some studies may involve direct interviews, expert consultations, client introductions or identifiable professional opinions. If your identity, professional profile, voice or identifiable quotations may be shared, we will explain the relevant purpose, recipients and use, and obtain any additional consent required.

Joining the panel does not automatically authorise the disclosure of your identifiable profile to clients for unrelated marketing.

12. Professional Recordings, Interviews and Quotations

Some research activities may involve telephone interviews, video calls, online focus groups, expert discussions, screen sharing or recorded sessions.

Before recording begins, we will provide appropriate information about:

The purpose of the recording.

The information or media being captured.

Whether your name, speciality, employer, voice or image may be identifiable.

Who may access the recording, transcript or quotations.

Whether excerpts may appear in research reports or presentations.

Relevant retention and deletion arrangements.

We will obtain separate consent where required.

You must not display patient records, confidential workplace systems or restricted documents during screen sharing unless you are authorised and the study permits it.

You may decline an optional recorded activity. An unrecorded alternative may not be available for every study.

13. Conflicts of Interest and Professional Ethics

You are responsible for following applicable professional codes, workplace policies, confidentiality obligations and legal requirements.

You should disclose relevant conflicts of interest where requested, including relationships that could materially affect your responses or eligibility for a particular study.

You must not:

Disclose confidential information obtained through your professional role without authorisation.

Use the panel to obtain restricted information about patients, colleagues or competing organisations.

Misrepresent a commercial relationship or professional qualification.

Participate in a study in a manner that violates applicable professional or legal requirements.

Treat research participation as permission to provide clinical services or make unauthorised medical claims.

If you believe a study conflicts with your professional obligations, contact the research team or decline participation.

14. Incentives, Honoraria and Payments

Eligible studies may offer points, cash payments, gift cards, vouchers or professional honoraria.

The applicable invitation will explain the reward, completion requirements, eligibility conditions and payment arrangements.

Incentives are subject to the study’s stated validation and approval conditions.

Payment methods and processing times may vary by country and study.

You must provide accurate redemption and payment information.

Suspected fraudulent or duplicate claims may be investigated.

Any withholding or adjustment of an incentive must be justified and handled in accordance with the published rules and applicable law.

You are responsible for complying with applicable tax requirements and any professional or employer rules governing the acceptance of honoraria.

You should determine whether your employer, healthcare institution or professional code permits you to accept the particular incentive before participating.

Where necessary, we may ask you to confirm that accepting the incentive is permitted.

We do not guarantee a minimum number of paid studies or a particular income.

Specific reward thresholds, expiry rules, cancellation conditions and redemption procedures will be described in the applicable Reward Policy or study invitation.

15. Privacy, Cookies and Technical Information

We process personal information in accordance with our Privacy Notice and applicable data protection laws.

Essential cookies and similar technologies may be used for login sessions, security and website functionality.

Where required, optional analytics and tracking technologies will be activated only after obtaining the appropriate consent. You may manage available preferences through the Cookie Notice or cookie settings.

We may use proportionate security checks to prevent unauthorised access, fraudulent participation and misuse of incentives.

We will provide additional information and obtain any consent required before using sensitive personal data, optional tracking identifiers or other specially protected information.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country and applicable law, you may have the right to:

Access your personal information.

Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.

Request deletion of eligible personal information.

Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Object to or restrict certain processing.

Request a portable copy of certain information.

Opt out of optional marketing communications.

Manage optional cookie preferences.

Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact support@myhealthopinions.com.

We may need to verify your identity before responding. Certain records may be retained where legally required or where another lawful basis applies, such as accounting, security, legal claims or dispute resolution.

17. Account Security and Acceptable Use

You must keep your login credentials secure, provide accurate information and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access.

You must not:

Create unauthorised duplicate accounts or impersonate another healthcare professional.

Falsify professional qualifications, registration numbers or clinical experience.

Use automated systems to submit survey responses.

Manipulate eligibility, quotas, quality checks or incentives.

Upload unlawful, infringing or malicious material.

Disclose restricted survey materials or confidential client information.

Attempt to access another participant’s account or restricted research data.

Submit patient information without the required authority and safeguards.

Harass other participants, researchers, clients or support personnel.

We may investigate suspected violations and take proportionate action, including excluding submissions, restricting rewards or suspending an account where justified and permitted by law.

18. Intellectual Property and Research Materials

Questionnaires, study designs, sponsor materials, software, product information and other research content may be protected by intellectual property rights or confidentiality obligations.

You may use these materials only for the authorised purpose of participating in the relevant study.

Unless authorised, you must not reproduce, publish, distribute or disclose confidential study materials.

You retain rights in your personal information as provided by applicable law. Any licence or permission concerning comments or materials you submit will be limited to the purposes described in the relevant study notice and applicable agreement.

Nothing in these terms transfers ownership of your employer’s intellectual property or authorises disclosure of information you do not have the right to share.

19. Withdrawal and Account Closure

You may stop participating in studies and request account closure at any time, subject to applicable law and the practical requirements of a study already completed.

We will delete or anonymise eligible personal information in accordance with our retention policy and applicable law.

Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, regulatory or dispute-resolution purposes.

Pending incentives and redemptions will be handled under the applicable reward rules and law.

You may withdraw a particular consent separately where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

20. Data Retention and International Transfers

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ for professional verification records, survey responses, incentive transactions, consent records and security logs.

Our research activities may involve clients and service providers in different countries. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will apply appropriate safeguards where required and provide relevant information about those arrangements.

We will maintain reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

21. Minors and Special Eligibility Requirements

The general Healthcare Professionals Panel is intended for eligible professionals who meet the applicable minimum age requirements.

Where a particular research activity has additional legal, professional or regulatory eligibility requirements, those requirements will be disclosed before participation.

We will not knowingly enrol individuals who fail the applicable age or consent requirements. Additional safeguards will apply where required by law.

22. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms to reflect changes in our services, research practices, security arrangements or legal requirements.

Where a change materially affects your rights or how your personal information is used, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain renewed consent where required.

Continued use of the panel will not replace a separate consent where the law requires affirmative agreement.

23. Service Availability, Liability and Complaints

We will take reasonable steps to maintain our panel services, but temporary interruptions may occur because of maintenance, technical problems, security incidents or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any right, remedy or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Any permitted limitations will be interpreted according to applicable law.

We will review complaints and respond in accordance with applicable requirements.
