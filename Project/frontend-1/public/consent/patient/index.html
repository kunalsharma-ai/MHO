1. Welcome to Our Patients Panel

Welcome to the Frequent Research Patients Panel, a research community for eligible individuals who wish to share their healthcare experiences, opinions, preferences and perspectives to help improve the understanding of patient needs and healthcare services.

Our research may cover:

Patient experiences and satisfaction with healthcare services.

Experiences of living with health conditions or disabilities.

Medicines, treatments and treatment experiences.

Medical devices, diagnostic tools and healthcare technologies.

Hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and other healthcare providers.

Patient support programmes and care services.

Healthcare access, affordability and patient communication.

Quality of life and the impact of health conditions on daily activities.

Digital health applications, remote care and patient portals.

Healthcare products, concepts and services under development.

Patient education, support resources and treatment-related information.

Participation may involve online surveys, telephone interviews, online discussions, product evaluations, focus groups or other research activities.

Membership is voluntary. Joining the panel does not make Frequent Research your healthcare provider, establish a doctor–patient relationship or guarantee access to treatment, medical services, financial assistance or healthcare benefits.

2. Eligibility and Membership

To join the panel, you must meet the applicable minimum age requirement and provide accurate registration information.

Depending on the study, eligibility may require:

Being a patient or having relevant personal experience with a particular health condition, treatment or healthcare service.

Having experience with a specified medicine, medical device, diagnostic procedure or healthcare provider.

Meeting the age, location or other eligibility requirements stated in the study invitation.

Providing appropriate confirmation of eligibility where reasonably necessary and lawful.

Meeting additional study-specific requirements communicated before participation.

You must not falsely claim a diagnosis, treatment experience, medical history or other qualification to gain access to a study.

Where verification is required, we will use proportionate methods and explain what information is needed and why. We will not routinely require complete medical records merely because you register for the panel.

Membership does not guarantee eligibility for every study, a minimum number of invitations or payment for participation.

3. Information We May Collect

We collect information relevant to panel membership, research eligibility and the purposes explained in our Privacy Notice.

A. Registration and contact information

Depending on your registration and country, this may include:

Full name or preferred display name.

Email address and telephone number.

Country, state, city or general location.

Preferred language and communication preferences.

Age or date of birth where necessary for eligibility.

Account credentials and security information.

B. General patient profile information

Depending on the study, we may ask about:

General age range and relevant demographic information.

Healthcare access and use of healthcare services.

General treatment or care experiences.

Relevant health conditions or patient experience categories.

Experiences with medicines, devices or diagnostic services.

General healthcare preferences and support needs.

Experience with hospitals, clinics, pharmacies or other providers.

We will seek only information reasonably necessary for the stated research purpose.

C. Health and medical information

Some studies may ask about sensitive health information, including:

Health conditions or diagnoses.

Symptoms and their general duration.

Treatment history and treatment experiences.

Medicines used and experiences with them.

Medical devices, diagnostic procedures or therapies.

Hospitalisation or interactions with healthcare professionals.

Physical functioning, wellbeing and quality of life.

Side effects, treatment challenges or unmet healthcare needs.

Other health-related information relevant to a particular study.

Health information is sensitive and may receive additional legal protection. Before collecting it, we will provide appropriate information about the study and obtain explicit consent or establish another lawful basis where required.

Joining the panel does not automatically authorise the collection of every category of health information.

D. Research responses

We may collect your answers, opinions, ratings, feedback and experiences through surveys, interviews, questionnaires or other research methods.

Some research may involve open-ended questions. You should share only the information necessary to answer the question and should avoid including personal information about other patients or healthcare professionals unless the study specifically permits it.

E. Technical and security information

We may collect IP address, device and browser details, login timestamps, session information, essential cookies, survey progress and response-quality indicators.

We may use proportionate security measures to detect duplicate accounts, unauthorised access, automated submissions and suspected fraudulent activity.

Optional analytics and tracking technologies will be handled according to applicable law and your cookie preferences.

F. Incentive and payment information

Where a study offers points, gift cards, vouchers, cash incentives or other rewards, we may collect reward preferences, redemption records and payment information required to administer them.

Tax or identity verification information will be requested where appropriate and legally required.

G. Medical documents and supporting evidence

Certain studies may require limited evidence to verify eligibility. If so, the study instructions will explain what evidence is acceptable, why it is required and how it will be handled.

Do not upload full medical records, prescriptions, laboratory reports, medical images or other documents unless the study expressly requests them through an authorised process.

Where possible, use an approved method that limits disclosure to the minimum information needed. Do not alter documents in a way that misrepresents eligibility or medical information.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

Register and manage your panel membership.

Determine eligibility for relevant healthcare research studies.

Match you with appropriate research invitations.

Conduct patient experience and healthcare market research.

Understand patient preferences, challenges and unmet needs.

Evaluate healthcare services, medicines, medical devices and technologies where appropriate.

Analyse research responses and prepare findings.

Validate response quality and prevent duplicate or fraudulent participation.

Administer eligible incentives and payments.

Communicate research invitations, account notices and support updates.

Improve our panel services and research processes.

Meet applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

Protect the security and integrity of our research services.

Research findings may help clients understand patient experiences and improve products, services or healthcare strategies. However, we do not guarantee that a study will result in a particular product change, treatment improvement or healthcare outcome.

We will not use your information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, providing notice or obtaining additional consent.

5. Voluntary Participation and Your Right to Decline

Your participation in the panel and in individual studies is voluntary.

You may decline research invitations and, where the study permits, skip questions or stop participating.

You agree to:

Provide truthful responses based on your own experiences.

Participate only in studies for which you are eligible.

Follow the applicable study instructions.

Avoid knowingly submitting false or misleading information.

Respect the confidentiality of restricted research materials.

Avoid disclosing another person’s private health information without appropriate authority.

You do not have to participate in every study. You may decline a question if you are uncomfortable answering it, subject to the eligibility and completion rules of the particular study.

Choosing not to participate will not affect your ordinary access to healthcare services, your relationship with your healthcare provider or any treatment to which you are otherwise entitled.

The panel is not an emergency service. If you need urgent medical assistance, contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service in your location.

6. Health Information Privacy and Confidentiality

Your health information must be handled with appropriate confidentiality and safeguards.

We will apply reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

Depending on the study, safeguards may include access restrictions, secure storage, limited data collection, separation of contact details from research responses and the use of pseudonymised or anonymised information.

However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should not include unnecessary personal identifiers in free-text responses or upload documents that contain more information than the study requires.

We will handle health information in accordance with the applicable Privacy Notice, study-specific information and relevant legal requirements.

7. Medical Records and Information About Other People

You must not submit identifiable information about another patient, family member or healthcare professional unless the study has been specifically designed and authorised to collect it and all applicable requirements have been met.

Unless expressly authorised through an appropriate study process, do not include:

Another person’s full name or contact information.

Medical record numbers or patient identification numbers.

Identifiable prescriptions, laboratory reports or medical images.

Photographs or recordings that identify another person.

Detailed medical histories belonging to another individual.

Confidential information obtained from a healthcare provider or medical record.

Information that could reasonably identify another patient.

When describing an experience involving a healthcare provider or another individual, use general descriptions wherever possible.

If you accidentally submit identifiable information that should not have been shared, notify the research team promptly. We may restrict access, remove or securely handle the information as appropriate.

8. No Medical Advice or Treatment

The Patients Panel is a research service, not a medical consultation platform.

Information presented in a questionnaire, study invitation, product evaluation or research discussion is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

You must not rely on panel materials to diagnose a condition, change a prescribed treatment, stop taking medication or make an urgent healthcare decision.

Unless a particular study explicitly states otherwise and has the required authorisations, research participation does not provide access to treatment, medical monitoring, diagnosis, prescriptions or clinical care.

Frequent Research and its research clients do not guarantee a particular health outcome from participation.

9. Medicines, Medical Devices and Healthcare Product Research

Some studies may ask about medicines, medical devices, diagnostic tools, therapies, healthcare applications or other products and services.

You agree to:

Describe your genuine experience and opinions.

Follow the instructions provided for the study.

Avoid making knowingly false statements about a product or treatment.

Avoid sharing another person’s confidential health information.

Use products only as directed by their authorised instructions and your healthcare professional.

Report safety concerns through the appropriate channel where the study provides one.

Unless a study has specifically been authorised for clinical research, product evaluation does not constitute a clinical trial or an instruction to use an experimental treatment.

You should not begin, discontinue or change any treatment because of your participation in a market research study.

10. Adverse Events, Side Effects and Product Complaints

Some medicine, vaccine, medical device or healthcare product studies may ask about side effects, adverse events, product complaints or device malfunctions.

Where relevant, the study instructions will explain how such information should be reported and whether additional follow-up may be required.

If you report a possible adverse event or product safety concern:

Follow the study-specific reporting instructions.

Provide only the information reasonably required for the authorised reporting process.

Avoid including unnecessary identifying information about other people.

Understand that relevant information may need to be passed to an authorised study sponsor, manufacturer or safety team where legally required.

The panel’s general support team is not a substitute for medical advice, emergency services or an authorised pharmacovigilance reporting channel.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, seek immediate assistance through the appropriate local emergency service.

11. Research Clients, Sponsors and Information Sharing

We may conduct research for pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, hospitals, healthcare providers, diagnostic companies, digital health businesses, research agencies and other legitimate research clients.

Where necessary and lawful, information may be shared with:

Research clients and study sponsors.

Authorised research agencies and project partners.

Survey hosting and panel management providers.

Cloud hosting, security and technical support providers.

Incentive and payment providers.

Auditors, professional advisers and public authorities where legally required.

We will apply appropriate safeguards to information handled by service providers acting on our behalf.

Where possible and appropriate, research outputs will use aggregated, statistical, pseudonymised or anonymised information.

In some studies, authorised researchers may need access to identifiable information for a clearly explained purpose. Before such access or disclosure, we will provide appropriate information and obtain any additional consent required.

Joining the panel does not automatically authorise the disclosure of your identifiable health information to clients for unrelated marketing purposes.

12. Anonymised and Pseudonymised Research Data

Research findings may be analysed and reported in a form that does not directly identify individual participants.

Anonymised information is information that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual, taking account of the applicable legal standard and available means of identification.

Pseudonymised information uses a code or other substitute for direct identifiers but may still be linked back to an individual using additional information. It therefore remains subject to applicable data protection requirements.

We will not describe information as anonymous merely because names have been removed.

Where research outputs include quotations or detailed patient stories that could identify you, we will apply appropriate safeguards and obtain additional consent where required.

13. Audio, Video, Interviews and Patient Stories

Some research may involve recorded interviews, online focus groups, video calls, voice recordings, photographs or patient experience stories.

Before a recording or identifiable story is collected, we will explain:

The purpose of the activity.

The information or media being captured.

Whether your name, voice, image or health experience may be identifiable.

Who may access the recording or transcript.

Whether excerpts may be included in research reports or presentations.

The intended retention and deletion arrangements.

We will obtain separate consent where required.

You may decline optional recorded activities. An unrecorded alternative may not be available for every study.

You must not record other participants or share their contributions outside the study unless authorised.

14. Incentives, Rewards and Payments

Eligible studies may offer points, cash incentives, gift cards, vouchers or other rewards.

The invitation will explain the applicable incentive and completion requirements.

Rewards are subject to the stated eligibility and validation conditions.

Payment methods and processing times may vary by country and study.

You must provide accurate redemption and payment information.

Suspected fraudulent or duplicate claims may be investigated.

Any withholding or adjustment of an incentive must be justified and handled in accordance with the published rules and applicable law.

Tax obligations will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Participation does not guarantee a minimum number of paid studies or a particular income.

Reward thresholds, expiry rules, redemption procedures and processing timelines will be described in the applicable Reward Policy or study invitation.

15. Privacy, Cookies and Technical Information

We process personal information according to our Privacy Notice and applicable data protection laws.

Essential cookies and similar technologies may be used for login sessions, security and website functionality.

Where required, optional analytics or tracking technologies will be activated only after obtaining appropriate consent. You may manage available preferences through our cookie settings.

We may use proportionate security measures to prevent unauthorised access, duplicate accounts and fraudulent participation.

Additional information and permissions will be provided where required for sensitive health information, optional tracking or other specially protected information.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country and applicable law, you may have the right to:

Access your personal information.

Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.

Request deletion of eligible personal information.

Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Object to or restrict certain processing.

Request a portable copy of certain information.

Opt out of optional marketing communications.

Manage optional cookie preferences.

Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact support@myhealthopinions.com.

We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some records may be retained where legally required or where another lawful basis applies, such as accounting, security, regulatory obligations or dispute resolution.

17. Account Security and Acceptable Use

You must keep your login credentials secure, provide accurate information and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access.

You must not:

Create unauthorised duplicate accounts or impersonate another person.

Falsify diagnoses, treatment histories or research eligibility.

Use automated systems to submit survey responses.

Manipulate study eligibility, quotas or incentive systems.

Upload unlawful, infringing or malicious material.

Disclose confidential research materials without permission.

Access another participant’s account or restricted research data.

Submit another person’s medical records without appropriate authority.

Harass participants, researchers, clients or support personnel.

We may investigate suspected violations and take proportionate action, including excluding submissions, restricting rewards or suspending an account where justified and permitted by law.

18. Intellectual Property and Research Materials

Survey questionnaires, study designs, client materials, software and other research content may be protected by intellectual property rights or confidentiality obligations.

You may use these materials only for the authorised purpose of participating in the relevant study.

Unless authorised, you must not reproduce, publish, distribute or disclose confidential research materials.

You retain rights in your personal information as provided by applicable law. Any licence or permission concerning comments, feedback or other material you submit will be limited to the purposes described in the relevant study notice and applicable agreement.

Nothing in these terms authorises the disclosure of confidential information belonging to another person or organisation.

19. Withdrawal and Account Closure

You may stop participating in research and request account closure at any time, subject to applicable law and the practical requirements of a study already completed.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent as described in the Privacy Notice.

We will delete or anonymise eligible personal information according to our retention policy and applicable law.

Certain records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security, regulatory or dispute-resolution purposes.

Research data that has been irreversibly anonymised may no longer be reasonably identifiable or removable from completed analyses. We will explain relevant limitations where required.

Pending incentives and redemptions will be handled under the applicable reward rules and law.

Withdrawal does not invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

20. Data Retention and International Transfers

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, subject to applicable legal, regulatory and operational requirements.

Retention periods may differ for account information, health-related research responses, consent records, incentive transactions and security logs.

Our research activities may involve clients and service providers in different countries. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will apply appropriate safeguards where required and provide relevant information about those arrangements.

We maintain reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

21. Minors and Research Involving Children

The general Patients Panel is intended for participants who meet the applicable minimum age requirements.

Research involving children or individuals who may lack the legal capacity to consent requires additional safeguards and appropriate authorisation under applicable law.

Where a study permits participation by minors, we will implement the required parental or guardian permissions, age-appropriate information, assent procedures where applicable and any necessary ethics or regulatory approvals.

A parent, guardian or family member must not assume that general panel membership authorises research involving a child’s or another person’s health information.

These general terms do not replace any study-specific consent or safeguarding requirements.

22. Clinical Trials and Regulated Medical Research

The general Patients Panel is primarily intended for patient experience research and healthcare market research.

A study that constitutes a clinical trial, regulated medical investigation or other specially regulated activity may require separate informed consent, ethics committee review, regulatory authorisation, a clinical study protocol and additional participant protections.

Panel registration or acceptance of these terms does not constitute consent to participate in a clinical trial.

Before any such study begins, the responsible study team must provide the required information and obtain the necessary approvals and participant consent.

23. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms to reflect changes in our services, research practices, security arrangements or legal requirements.

Where a change materially affects your rights or how your personal information is used, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain renewed consent where required.

Continued use of the panel will not replace a separate consent where the law requires affirmative agreement.

24. Service Availability, Liability and Complaints

We will take reasonable steps to maintain our panel services, but temporary interruptions may occur because of maintenance, technical issues, security incidents or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any right, remedy or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. Any permitted limitations will be interpreted according to applicable law.

For questions, complaints or requests, contact:

We will review complaints and respond in accordance with applicable requirements.

By selecting the relevant consent options, you confirm that you have had the opportunity to read these terms and the Privacy Notice and understand the choices presented to you.

Your permissions apply only to the purposes described in the relevant notice and the options you select. Optional permissions may be declined or withdrawn where applicable without automatically cancelling your general panel membership.

A particular study may require additional consent for sensitive health information, medical documents, recordings, identifiable quotations, clinical research or other specially regulated activities.

These general panel terms do not replace those documents or any required ethics committee, regulatory or clinical research approvals.
